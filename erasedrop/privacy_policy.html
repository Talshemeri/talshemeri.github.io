# Privacy Policy for EraseDrop **Last updated:** September 12, 2026 This Privacy Policy describes how EraseDrop ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") handles your information when you use our iOS application. ## 1. Overview EraseDrop is a photo editing app that removes unwanted objects and backgrounds from your photos. Privacy is a core part of how the App is designed: **all photo processing happens entirely on your device**. Your photos are never uploaded to our servers or to any third party for editing purposes. ## 2. Information We Do Not Collect We do not collect, store, or transmit: - Your photos, images, or any edits you make to them - Your name, email address, or other personal identifiers (unless you contact us directly, see Section 5) - Your precise location All image processing (background removal, object erasure, upscaling, and related features) is performed using on-device machine learning models. Your photos never leave your device as part of using the App's core editing features. ## 3. Advertising EraseDrop offers a free tier supported by rewarded video ads. If you choose not to subscribe, you may be shown a rewarded ad (via Google AdMob) in exchange for unlocking actions such as saving an edited photo. We use **Google AdMob** to display these ads. AdMob may collect certain data automatically to serve and measure ads, which can include: - Device identifiers (such as your advertising ID) - IP address and general location (derived from IP) - App usage data related to ad interactions (impressions, clicks, completions) This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's own privacy policy. You can review it here: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) You may be able to limit ad tracking through your iOS device settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising). On iOS, the App will request your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before any tracking-related identifiers are used, where applicable. ## 4. Photo Library Access EraseDrop requests access to your photo library solely so you can select photos to edit and save your edited results back to your library. This access is used only on your device and is never transmitted to us. ## 5. Contact If you contact us directly (for example, by email for support), we will only use the information you provide (such as your email address) to respond to your inquiry. We do not use this information for any other purpose and do not share it with third parties. Contact: **talshemeri95@gmail.com** ## 6. Children's Privacy EraseDrop is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the App does not collect personal data as part of its core functionality, this risk is minimal, but the advertising SDK described in Section 3 is subject to Google's own policies regarding child-directed treatment where applicable. ## 7. Data Retention Because we do not collect or store your photos or personal data on our servers, there is no server-side data of yours for us to retain or delete. Any data processed by AdMob is retained according to Google's own data retention practices. ## 8. Your Rights Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding any personal data processed by third-party services like Google AdMob, including the right to access, correct, or delete such data, and the right to opt out of certain data processing. Please refer to Google's privacy policy and your device's privacy settings to exercise these rights. ## 9. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this document. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. ## 10.
