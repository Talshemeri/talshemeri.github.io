Privacy Policy AI Studio

Developer: El Maravilla · App: AI Studio (com.elmaravilla.aistudio) · Last updated: March 7, 2026

✅ Short version: Your photos never leave your device. We don't sell your data. On-device processing means your images are never uploaded to any server by us.

📋 1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Studio ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our iOS application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

By using AI Studio, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the app.

🛡️ 2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your photos, images, or any media you edit
  • Your name, email address, or any contact information
  • Your location data
  • Biometric data of any kind
  • Device contacts, calendars, or messages

All photo processing is performed entirely on your device using on-device machine learning models. Your images are never uploaded to our servers — because we have none.

📷 3. Photos & Camera Access

AI Studio requests access to your photo library solely to allow you to select images for editing. This permission is used exclusively on-device and only when you actively choose an image.

We do not read, copy, transmit, or back up your photo library. Photos remain on your device at all times.

📊 4. Analytics & Crash Reporting

We may collect anonymous, aggregated crash reports through Apple's standard crash-reporting frameworks (e.g., MetricKit / Xcode Organizer) to improve app stability.

  • Crash reports do not include personally identifiable information
  • Reports contain only technical diagnostics (stack traces, device type, iOS version)
  • You can opt out at the device level via Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements
  • Crash data is retained for up to 90 days and then permanently deleted

📣 5. Advertising — Google AdMob

AI Studio displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use certain data to serve ads, independent of our own data practices. This may include:

  • Device identifiers (IDFA / advertising ID)
  • IP address (used for approximate location)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, taps)
  • Device type, operating system, and language settings
⚠️ App Tracking Transparency: In accordance with Apple's ATT framework, AI Studio will ask for your permission before allowing AdMob to access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) for personalized ads. If you decline, only non-personalized (contextual) ads will be shown. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

AdMob's data practices are governed by Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

To opt out of personalized advertising by Google: adssettings.google.com

💳 6. In-App Purchases — Apple StoreKit

In-app purchases (Yearly subscription and Lifetime access) are processed entirely by Apple's StoreKit. We do not collect, see, or store your payment information.

Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy: apple.com/legal/privacy

  • Yearly plan: Auto-renews annually at the price shown at purchase. Cancel anytime in your App Store subscription settings.
  • Lifetime plan: A one-time, non-consumable purchase. No recurring charges.

👶 7. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

AI Studio is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at support@aiphotos.app and we will take steps to remove that information.

Because the app includes advertising via AdMob, it is rated 4+ and parents should be aware that ads shown may include general audience content.

⚖️ 8. Your Rights

EU / EEA Users (GDPR): You have the right to access, correct, and request deletion of any personal data we hold about you. Since we collect no personal data directly, there is nothing to access or delete on our end. For AdMob data, contact Google directly.

California Residents (CCPA): We do not sell personal information. You have the right to know what personal information is collected and to request deletion. Contact us at support@aiphotos.app for any requests.

🗓️ 9. Data Retention

  • Photos & edits: Never stored by us. Exist only on your device.
  • Crash reports: Retained for up to 90 days, then deleted.
  • Purchase records: Managed by Apple; governed by Apple's policies.
  • Ad data: Managed by Google AdMob; governed by Google's policies.

🔒 10. Security

Because all processing occurs on-device and we do not operate servers that store your data, the primary security layer is your device itself. We encourage you to keep your device updated and use a passcode or Face ID/Touch ID.

🔔 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will notify users via an in-app notice or App Store update notes.

Continued use of AI Studio after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
